About

From embedded foundations to connected systems.

I am an Electrical Engineering graduate from College of Engineering, Pune. My early experience at Robot Study Circle built my practical foundation in debugging and embedded systems, leading to my current work at Bajaj Auto Technology Ltd.

My work spans automotive operating-system development, telematics modem software, legacy-code maintenance and debugging. I enjoy understanding systems end-to-end rather than treating software as an isolated layer.
